Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Beam does not provide user accounts and does not store gameplay progress on DyxotaGame servers.

Request deletion from DyxotaGame

  1. Email dyxotapls@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Beam Data Deletion Request.
  3. State whether your request concerns support correspondence, advertising data, or both.
  4. Include only the information reasonably needed to locate the relevant records. Do not send passwords, payment details, or government identifiers.

We may reply to verify that the request came from the email address associated with the relevant support correspondence. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, unless applicable law permits or requires additional time.

Data that can be deleted

  • Support correspondence: messages and attachments you previously sent to DyxotaGame that remain under our control.
  • Advertising-related requests: we will assist with a request concerning data processed through Yandex Mobile Ads and may direct or forward the request to the provider where necessary.

Local gameplay data

Level progress, hint balances, and Game settings are stored locally on your Android device. DyxotaGame cannot remotely access or delete this local data. You can delete it immediately by using either method:

  • open Android Settings → Apps → Beam → Storage → Clear storage; or
  • uninstall Beam from your device.

Data that may be retained

After a verified deletion request is completed, residual copies of support correspondence may remain in restricted backups for up to 90 days before being overwritten. We may retain limited records for longer when reasonably necessary to comply with law, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Advertising information controlled by Yandex is subject to Yandex’s retention practices and applicable legal obligations. See the Yandex Privacy Policy.
