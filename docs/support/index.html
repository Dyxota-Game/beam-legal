Beam Support

Support for the Android game Beam by DyxotaGame.

If you found a bug, have trouble with an advertisement or reward, or have a privacy question, email us at:

dyxotapls@gmail.com

What to include

  • a short description of what happened;
  • the steps that led to the problem;
  • your device model and Android version;
  • the Beam version shown in Google Play;
  • a screenshot, if it helps explain the issue.

Do not include passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, or other sensitive personal information.

Privacy requests

Use the same email address with the subject Beam Privacy Request. Describe whether your request concerns support correspondence, local game data, or advertising data. Local progress can be removed directly by clearing Beam’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the Game.

Response time

We aim to review support and privacy messages within 14 days. Complex privacy requests may take longer where permitted by applicable law.
