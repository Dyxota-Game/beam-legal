Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DyxotaGame (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the mobile game Beam (the “Game”), published under the Android package name com.dyxotagame.beam.

Beam does not require an account. We do not ask you to provide your name, address, phone number, or payment information inside the Game.

1. Information stored on your device

Beam stores gameplay progress and settings, such as completed levels, audio preferences, and similar game state, locally on your device. This information is used to provide and restore Game functionality. We do not operate a server that receives this locally stored game progress.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the Game’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the Game.

2. Advertising and third-party processing

Beam displays interstitial and rewarded advertisements using Yandex Mobile Ads, a third-party advertising service provided by Yandex. When advertisements are requested or shown, the advertising SDK may collect and share information with Yandex, including:

  • device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising identifier when available and permitted;
  • technical device and app information, such as device model, operating-system version, language, screen characteristics, app version, and IP address;
  • advertising interactions and delivery information, such as ad requests, impressions, clicks, and reward events;
  • limited diagnostic or performance information needed to deliver, measure, secure, and troubleshoot advertising services.

This information may be processed for advertising, ad measurement, service security, fraud prevention, and compliance. Data transmitted by Yandex Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Beam disables location tracking in the Yandex advertising SDK and does not request precise or approximate location for advertising. Beam currently requests non-personalized advertising. Your Android privacy and advertising settings may provide additional controls, including the ability to reset or delete the advertising identifier.

Yandex processes information under its own terms and policies. For further details, review the Yandex Privacy Policy and the Yandex Mobile Ads data-safety information.

3. Information you send to us

If you contact us for support or make a privacy request, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use it only to respond, investigate the issue, protect the Game, or comply with legal obligations. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

4. Legal bases and choices

Depending on where you live, processing may rely on consent, our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the Game, performance of a requested service, or compliance with law. Where consent is required, advertising-related processing should occur only after the applicable choice has been obtained. You may withdraw or change available choices through the Game’s privacy controls or your device settings.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear the Game’s storage or uninstall it. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to answer your request, resolve disputes, maintain security, and meet legal requirements.

Advertising information is retained by Yandex according to its policies. You may email us to request access to or deletion of information we control, or for assistance with a request involving the advertising service. We may need reasonable information to verify and locate the relevant records. See our Data Deletion Instructions for the request steps, affected data, and retention details.

6. Children’s privacy

Beam is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

7. International processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their processing is governed by their policies and applicable safeguards.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information we handle. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its services, or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated inside the Game or through the store listing when appropriate.
